# Timetable and route geometry

The schedule, stops and shape are by Prague Integrated Transport (PID / ROPID).
PID offers its downloadable datasets under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Dataset description and license notice](https://pid.cz/en/opendata/) · [Official rolling GTFS archive](https://data.pid.cz/PID_GTFS.zip)

The archive served on 23 November 2026 had a last-modified timestamp of 2026-11-23 03:10:44 UTC and SHA-256 `78ab051f2525395fa8aafe51e63eb797b89db30c6eb622170d944c00a21f324d`.

`prague-22.zip` is an extract of route `L22`, service `1111100-1`, trip `22_33955_260831`, shape `L22V4`. It retains the whole trip, its stops, shape and calendar records. Other trips, tables and optional columns were removed. The playable scene uses stop sequences 27–29, inclusive, on 2026-11-23. The importer projects the clipped shape into local metres and places the stop markers along it. The minute-by-minute animation between timetable entries is an estimate.

The scenery and tram model are original, stylized artwork. The timetable, extract and derived route geometry retain their data license.
